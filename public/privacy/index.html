Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through the contact form on this site. The site is run by Vipul M.R, who decides how those details are used.

What the form collects

The details you type in: usually your name, your email address and your message, plus any other fields the form shows. Nothing else is collected through the form.

Why

Only to answer your message and, if you ask for it, to work with you. Your details are not used for newsletters or advertising and are not sold.

Who processes it

The contact form is delivered by Web3Forms, operated by Web3Creative. It processes the information you submit to deliver your enquiry to Vipul's email inbox. Web3Forms acts as a data processor on Vipul's behalf. For more information, see the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

How long it is kept

Web3Forms retains form submission data for up to three years by default, subject to its applicable retention settings and any earlier deletion. Vipul keeps his own copy of correspondence only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and manage any work that follows.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent, or object to how they are used. Write to phobosanderos@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
